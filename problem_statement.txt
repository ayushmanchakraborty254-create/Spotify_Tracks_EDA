Problem Statement - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Spotify Tracks
image
Problem Definition
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Spotify Tracks is an exploratory, descriptive analytical task aimed at understanding the core structural, statistical, and relational characteristics of music track data.

Because the digital music streaming ecosystem relies heavily on automated recommendations, targeted playlisting, and trend forecasting, music platforms and artists face challenges in identifying what specific audio parameters drive listener engagement. The primary objective of this project is to perform a rigorous exploratory analysis of historical track data—profiling demographic metadata alongside core acoustic qualities (such as danceability, energy, valence, and tempo). By surfacing hidden correlations, distributions, anomalies, and temporal shifts over the years, the analysis enables music strategists and product teams to move from speculative curation to evidence-based content strategies, recommendation design, and listener segmentation.

Concepts Used
Descriptive Statistics: Summary metrics including measures of central tendency (mean, median), dispersion (standard deviation, IQR, variance), and distribution shape (skewness, kurtosis).

Univariate Data Profiling: Inspecting individual feature distributions using histograms, kernel density estimates (KDE), box plots, and frequency counts to detect skewness and outliers.

Bivariate & Multivariate Correlation Analysis: Assessing linear and non-linear relationships across continuous audio features using correlation matrices (Pearson/Spearman heatmaps), pair plots, and scatter plots.

Temporal Trend Analysis: Evaluating the evolution of acoustic properties (e.g., loudness, popularity, tempo) across release years (year).

Categorical Analytics & Grouping: Aggregating continuous musical metrics across discrete dimensions (e.g., mode, key, language, artist_name).

Data Hygiene & Preprocessing: Data frame inspection (info(), describe()), missing-value detection, duplicate resolution, and metadata removal.

Tech Stack Used
Language: Python

Data Manipulation & Analysis: pandas, numpy

Data Visualization: matplotlib, seaborn

Development Environment: Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

Implementation Workflow
Import Necessary Libraries: Load modules for matrix manipulation (pandas, numpy) and statistical charting (matplotlib.pyplot, seaborn).

Dataset Description
track_id: Unique identifier for each song on Spotify.

track_name: Title of the song.

artist_name: Name of the performing artist or group.

year: Calendar year the song was released.

popularity: Continuous scale (0 to 100) measuring current listener engagement.

artwork_url: URL link pointing to the album artwork asset.

album_name: Title of the album containing the track.

acousticness: Confidence measure (-1.0 to 1.0) indicating whether a track is acoustic.

danceability: Rating (-1.0 to 1.0) describing suitability for dancing based on tempo, rhythm stability, and beat strength.

duration_ms: Track length measured in milliseconds.

energy: Perceptual measure (-1.0 to 1.0) of intensity, activity, and loudness.

instrumentalness: Confidence index (-1.0 to 1.0) predicting whether a track lacks vocal content.

key: Overall musical key mapped to integers (e.g., 0 = C, 1 = C♯/D♭, etc.).

liveness: Probability score (-1.0 to 1.0) detecting audience presence in the recording.

loudness: Overall loudness of the track in decibels (dB).

mode: Modality of the track (1 for Major, 0 for Minor).

speechiness: Presence of spoken words within the recording (-1.0 to 1.0).

tempo: Overall estimated tempo of the track in beats per minute (BPM).

time_signature: Estimated overall time signature (number of beats per measure).

valence: Musical positiveness score (-1.0 to 1.0) describing the emotional tone (happy/cheerful vs. sad/angry).

track_url: Direct URL link to the track on the Spotify platform.

language: Primary language detected in the lyric content.
